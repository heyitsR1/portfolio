World Cup 2026 predictor

numerical methods · python · westcliff bsit 400

Who wins the 2026 World Cup? A logistic regression trained on 49,000 international matches going back to 1872, then ten thousand Monte Carlo simulations of the tournament. No scikit-learn: the linear algebra is an LU solver, the regression is gradient descent, the decision threshold comes out of a Newton-Raphson root finder, and the features are ELO ratings computed from scratch.

Run main.py and it prints the top ten championship probabilities and saves the plots. Final project for Numerical Methods, and the reason I now actually understand what a solver does.

lu decomposition · gradient descent · newton-raphson · elo · monte carlo

Spotify valence explorer

ml · recommender · streamlit

When you like a track, finding others with the same feel is tedious and playlist radios are biased by popularity. This app takes a song and returns the ones closest to it in audio-feature space: valence, energy, acousticness, tempo. A nearest-neighbour index over a Kaggle dataset, with radar and heatmap views to see why two songs sit together. No Spotify API on purpose, to keep the focus on the model.

feature scaling · nearest neighbours · radar plots · eda notebooks
